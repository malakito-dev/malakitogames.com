How to play

Marco Polo

They shout “Marco…”, answer “Polo!” into the mic at the right volume so you’re not found. Whisper or scream, and they’ll find you.

Fill the Star Path

Step on the red stars on the floor. Fill them all to move on to the next room. But be careful, something is following your steps…

Use the Remote

Use the remote to turn walls and other objects. Bend the house in your favor to make your way through.

Solve Puzzles

Some rooms hold a puzzle… and something you might rather not remember.
