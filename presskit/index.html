About the game

Some memories won’t stay buried: they demand an encore. A short horror game where you play Marco Polo with your microphone... and someone is chasing you.

01

Marco Polo

Play Marco Polo with your microphone while your memories hunt you down: they call out “Marco...”, and you answer “Polo!” into your mic at just the right volume to stay hidden. Whisper or shout, and they’ll find you.

02

Fill the Star Path

Step on the red stars on the floor. Fill them all to reach the next room. But be careful, something is following your footsteps...

03

Use the Remote

Use the remote to rotate walls and other objects. Turn the house in your favor and clear your way.

04

Solve Puzzles

Some rooms hide a puzzle... and something you’d rather not remember.

About Malakito Games

Malakito Games is an independent one-person studio founded in 2026, and Encore House is its first game. The idea came up in a conversation about summers at the swimming pool and the games of Marco Polo we used to play there: what if that childhood game were the core of a horror game, and the one calling “Marco” were hunting you down?
